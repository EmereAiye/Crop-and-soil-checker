How a photo becomes a diagnosis

  1. Check what it is

    Leaf or soil is detected automatically. Hands, walls, sky and dark or blank photos are turned away with a tip.

  2. Read the colours

    Green, yellow, brown, red, grey and white areas are measured and the problem spots are marked on your photo.

  3. Match to known problems

    The pattern is compared with common crop diseases and soil conditions, and you get causes, steps and prevention.

Analysis runs in your browser, so your photo stays on your device. It reads colour and pattern, so it is a first guess, not a lab test. Soil colour cannot show pH or nutrient levels, and many diseases look alike, so confirm with your local agricultural extension officer before spraying or applying lime or fertiliser.
